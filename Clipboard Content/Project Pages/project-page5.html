Augmented Reality Tracking Experiences -Repository
This project explores how augmented reality can be used to reduce workplace or life stress through immersive, interactive experiences designed to promote relaxing emotions. Rather than removing users from their surroundings entirely, the application integrates calming digital interactions directly into the real world, allowing users to briefly disengage from stress while remaining aware of their environment. Developed in Unity using both ARCore and Vuforia, the application combines image target tracking, ground plane detection, and real-time lighting estimation to create believable and responsive AR interactions. The experience was designed around the idea that immersion and realism are essential to emotional engagement. By matching virtual object lighting to the user’s physical environment and anchoring content naturally within real-world spaces, the application aimed to create interactions that felt tangible and emotionally convincing. Unlike traditional relaxation applications that transport users into fully virtual environments, this approach leverages augmented reality to enhance the user’s existing surroundings rather than replace them. This design philosophy allows users to maintain awareness of their workspace while still benefiting from short, calming interactions that help redirect focus momentarily and reduce stress. The result is a more accessible and practical form of relaxation that can be integrated naturally into everyday routines without causing major disruption or distraction. A significant focus of the project was user experience design. Through multiple iterations and user testing, I learned that even small usability issues could break immersion and reduce the effectiveness of the experience. To address this, I implemented intuitive onboarding tutorials, improved UI legibility, and designed soft, rounded visual elements intended to evoke calming emotional responses. Careful consideration was also given to AR-specific limitations and behaviors, ensuring that interactions remained stable, understandable, and comfortable for users across different work and home environments. This project strengthened my skills in AR development, interaction design, and user-centered problem solving while demonstrating how emerging technologies can be applied to create meaningful, human-focused experiences.
